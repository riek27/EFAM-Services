About REDO

A Catalyst for Peace, Justice and Sustainable Change

REDO (Rural Education and Development Organization) is a South Sudanese non-governmental organization committed to empowering communities through integrated programs in peacebuilding, justice, education, livelihoods, and climate resilience. We partner with local leaders, government institutions, and international agencies to deliver context-responsive solutions that uphold human dignity and leave no one behind.

Our Mission

To empower marginalized communities through inclusive, sustainable, and rights-based development programs.

Our Vision

A peaceful, just and prosperous South Sudan where every individual thrives with dignity and opportunity.

Integrity

Transparency, accountability and ethical conduct in every action we take.

Inclusion

Centering women, youth, persons with disabilities and the most vulnerable.
